Privacy policy

Tangent, the daily calculus Set. Effective October 25, 2026.

Who runs Tangent

Tangent is made by Luc Haji, an individual developer in the United States. Contact: luchaji07@gmail.com.

The short version

Tangent has no accounts and no servers. Everything it keeps stays on your iPhone. Nothing you do in the app is sent to Luc or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

  • Your answers (which question, whether it was right, how long it took), your course and unit, your streak, and your settings.
  • A small summary of today (done or not, the streak) that the Tangent widget and the Gate read. It lives in the app's own shared storage on the phone.
  • If you import a personal question pack, its file.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. iCloud backups of your iPhone include this data the way they include any app's.

The Gate and Shortcuts

If you set up the Gate, Apple's Shortcuts app runs a Tangent action when you open the apps you chose. That happens on your phone; Tangent only learns that the action ran (to open your Set or let you through). Tangent can't see which apps you use beyond that.

Reminders

The daily reminder is a notification scheduled on your phone. No push service is involved.

Analytics

Tangent contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites. Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send through TestFlight.

Purchases

If you buy Tangent Pro, Apple handles the payment. Tangent sees only whether Pro is active on your device.

Children

Tangent is rated 4+ and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If Tangent ever starts sending any data off the phone, this page will say exactly what, before the app does it.
